Who coaching is for

You might be in the right place if any of this sounds familiar.

At a crossroads

You have options but can't seem to choose. Or you know you want something different but don't know what that looks like yet.

Feeling stuck

You're going through the motions but not moving forward. Something feels off and you can't quite name it.

Navigating a career or life shift

A job change, a move, a relationship shift. Big transitions feel easier when you have a clear-headed person in your corner.

Overwhelmed by too much

You have too many ideas, too many responsibilities or too many voices telling you what to do. Time to get clear on what actually matters.

Travelling and seeking direction

Travel gives you perspective, but sometimes you need a structured conversation to make sense of what you're discovering about yourself.

Just curious

You don't have a crisis. You're just someone who takes their own growth seriously and wants a thoughtful thinking partner.

What a session looks like

A conversation, not a lecture.

Coaching sessions with Kate are 60 minutes. They can happen online or in person if you're in the same location.

There's no homework, no workbook and no jargon. Just a focused conversation designed to help you think more clearly about whatever you're navigating.

Most people feel a shift within the first session. Some book a series. Some book one and come back months later. There's no pressure either way.

My approach

I ask the questions. You find the answers.

I don't believe in telling people what to do. You already have more answers than you realise — sometimes you just need the right questions to access them.

My coaching is grounded in ICF-accredited principles and draws on years of experience working with people across very different life situations. I'm direct, warm and — when needed — usefully challenging.
